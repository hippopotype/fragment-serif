These fonts are licensed under the hippopotype font license, version 2026-09: the agreement, the Desktop and Web schedules and the free-claim terms below. The same text is published at https://hippopotype.com/licensing.

## Font license agreement

Source: https://hippopotype.com/licensing/agreement

> The terms every hippopotype font license shares: who holds it, what it grants, what it never allows, and how it ends.

### Parties

> This agreement is between hippopotype, the seller named in the terms of sale, and the license holder named on the order. It applies to every font license bought or claimed on hippopotype.com, together with the schedule for each license category on the order. The receipt, or for a free claim the license certificate, records which schedules and tiers were granted and at which version of this text.

### License holder

> The license holder is the person or organization named in the license holder details at checkout. Only the license holder and the people it authorizes under a schedule may use the fonts. A person buying for an employer or a client names that employer or client as the license holder; buying under a personal name for company use is not permitted.

### Term

> The license is perpetual. It does not expire and there is no renewal fee. It ends only by termination under this agreement.

### Tiers

> Each schedule sets its scope in tiers. A tier grants its own allowance and every smaller allowance below it, so one license covers the smaller uses too. When a use grows past the tier on the order, the license moves to the tier that covers it and only the difference between the two tiers is paid; the same license continues for the same styles and the same license holder, and nothing is bought again. Moving up is arranged by contact where the store cannot do it.

### Licensed files

> The licensed files are the font files delivered for the styles and formats listed on the order. A license covers the styles listed on the order and no other style of the family.

### Grant

> hippopotype grants the license holder a non-exclusive, worldwide license to install and use the licensed files within the scope, tier and allowance of each schedule on the order. The license stays with the license holder and moves to anyone else only as the transfers section allows. Uses that no schedule on the order covers need a further license. hippopotype keeps all rights in the fonts that this agreement does not grant.

### Permitted outputs

> Anything the license holder makes with the fonts, such as documents, images, logos, videos, printed matter and rendered text, belongs to the license holder and may be used and distributed without limit. The restriction is on the font files themselves, never on the work made with them. Converting text to outlines or images does not need a further license.

### Restrictions

> The license holder may not redistribute, sell, rent, lend, sublicense or give away the font files, in whole or in part, in any format. The license holder may not modify the font files, convert them to other formats, or make derivative fonts, except where a schedule allows subsetting for delivery. The font files may not be placed where the public can download them, used to build a font-creation service, or used to train a model that generates fonts.

### Transfers

> The license may be transferred once, in full, to a successor of the license holder, for example after a merger or a sale of the business, by writing to hippopotype with both parties named. The previous holder deletes every copy after the transfer. Any other transfer needs written consent.

### Agencies, contractors and printers

> A contractor or agency working for the license holder may install the licensed files for that work only, counted against the license holder's allowance, and deletes them when the work ends. A printer, sign maker or similar service may receive the files for producing the license holder's own work. An agency that keeps the fonts for its own or other clients' work needs its own license naming the agency as holder.

### Changes and versions

> hippopotype may publish new versions of this agreement and of the schedules. A license is governed by the version recorded on its order, which the receipt or certificate shows. Where updated font files for a licensed style are delivered to the same account, they stay under the version on the original order.

### Termination

> The license ends automatically if the license holder breaks a restriction and does not cure the breach within 30 days of written notice. On termination the license holder deletes every copy of the licensed files. Work already made with the fonts stays with the license holder.

### Support and warranty

> hippopotype delivers the font files as described on the hub and replaces files that are defective or wrongly delivered, as the terms of sale set out. Beyond that the fonts are supplied as they are, and hippopotype is not liable for indirect losses arising from their use, to the extent the law allows. Questions go to hello@hippopotype.com.

### Governing law

> This agreement is governed by the law of Poland. If the license holder is a consumer in the European Union, the mandatory consumer protection rules of the country where the consumer lives still apply and nothing here takes them away. Disputes go to the competent court under those rules. Where the license holder is not a consumer, the court for disputes is the court competent for the seller's registered office in Warsaw, Poland.

## Desktop license

Source: https://hippopotype.com/licensing/desktop

> Install the fonts on computers and make logos, print, documents and static graphics.

### What it covers

> The Desktop license covers installing the OTF files on computers and using them in any application to make documents, presentations, logos, packaging, signage, illustrations and other static or printed work. Work made this way may be produced in any number of copies and any print run. It does not cover embedding the font files in websites, apps, devices or paid media; those have their own schedules.

### Counting users

> The tier is the number of people who may have the fonts installed, wherever they work and on however many of their own devices. Tier 1 covers 5 users. A shared computer counts as one user per person who uses the fonts on it. When the number of people grows past the tier, the license moves to the tier that covers them.

### Logos, print and static graphics

> Logos, wordmarks and brand assets made with the fonts belong to the license holder and may be registered as trademarks. Print of any run length is included. Static images for the web and for social media are included, because the font files never leave the license holder's computers.

### Document embedding

> The fonts may be embedded in PDF and similar documents for viewing and printing, and in editable documents shared inside the license holder's organization. Documents with the fonts embedded may not be built so that a recipient can extract or install the fonts.

### Agency and contractor use

> A freelancer, contractor or agency working for the license holder may install the fonts for that work; they count as users of the license holder's tier and delete the files when the work ends. An agency that wants the fonts for its own portfolio of clients licenses them in its own name.

### Files delivered

> OTF files for each licensed style. WOFF2 and TTF files are delivered under the Web and App licenses.

## Web license

Source: https://hippopotype.com/licensing/web

> Self-host the WOFF2 files on the license holder's own websites and web apps.

### What it covers

> The Web license covers serving the WOFF2 files from the license holder's own websites and web applications with CSS @font-face, so text renders in the fonts in the visitor's browser. It covers every site and domain the license holder owns, as long as the combined traffic stays inside the tier.

### Visitors or page views

> The tier is measured either as monthly unique visitors or as monthly page views across all sites using the fonts, whichever the license holder tracks. Tier 1 covers 5,000 monthly visitors or 20,000 monthly page views. Choose the reading at purchase or claim; the receipt or license certificate records it.

### Hosting and CDNs

> The files may be served from the license holder's own servers or from a CDN or hosting provider the license holder controls. Serving the files for other people's sites is not permitted. Reasonable technical measures, such as referrer checks or hashed file names, are encouraged but not required.

### Staging and development

> Development, staging and preview environments of licensed sites are included and do not count toward traffic.

### Web apps

> Web applications behind a login count their users as visitors. A web app that lets the public create and download work with the fonts, such as a design tool, is not covered here and needs a separate arrangement.

### When traffic grows

> When traffic grows past the tier, the license moves to the tier that covers it, as the agreement sets out.

### Files delivered

> WOFF2 files for each licensed style.

## Free claim terms

Source: https://hippopotype.com/licensing/free-claim

> The styles marked free on the font pages are free for Desktop and Web at tier 1, claimed in the store or downloaded from the official release, under the same agreement as a paid license.

### Which styles

> Each font page marks the styles it offers free. Every other style is paid at every tier.

### Which licenses

> A free claim grants the Desktop license and the Web license at tier 1 for the claimed styles: 5 users and 5,000 monthly visitors or 20,000 monthly page views. Higher tiers of Desktop and Web are bought like any other license. A claim grants no rights under the App, Advertising, Broadcasting or OEM schedules; those are bought separately for the same styles.

### Claiming

> A free license can be claimed from an account on hippopotype.com. The claim names a license holder like a purchase does, is recorded on a license certificate in the account, and the files are downloaded from the account. There is no payment card, no eligibility test and no declaration.

### Official download

> The styles marked free can also be downloaded from hippopotype's official GitHub release, linked from each font page. A copy from that release carries the same free license as a claim, without claiming: whoever uses it is the license holder, under the same rules on who may hold a license, and the release stands in for the order, including the version of these terms it names.

### Same agreement

> A free license, claimed or downloaded, is a full license under the font license agreement and the Desktop and Web schedules. It is perpetual and it is not a trial. Every permission and restriction applies exactly as it does to a paid license, including the rules on agencies, redistribution and modification.

### When more is needed

> When a claimed style is needed above tier 1, the license moves to the tier that covers it the same way as any other license, and the claim stays valid for its own scope. A use that the Desktop and Web schedules do not cover needs the matching license, bought in the store or arranged by contact.
